Our story

Built for the shop counter, not the boardroom

Small shops deserve billing that feels as easy as writing a bill, and works as smartly as a big store.— The idea behind EasyBill
THE PROBLEM

Notebooks, calculators and lost bills

At a busy counter, totals are added by hand, discounts are remembered by heart and credit lives in a notebook. One slip and the money is gone.

THE IDEA

One simple app any shopkeeper can use

No training, no heavy software. Pick products, take payment and hand over a clean bill from the phone already in the pocket.

THE BUILD

Everything a counter really needs

Retail and wholesale billing, stock, customers, UPI QR, WhatsApp sharing and a full bill history in a calm, uncluttered design.

TODAY

Trusted by 10+ customers and growing

EasyBill keeps getting simpler and smarter, shaped by the people who use it every day behind the counter.
